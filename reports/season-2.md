# Season 2

23 decks (7 meta, 8 incumbents, 8 new) × 24 games per pairing, deep bot, with telemetry. New decks were forged (1 gen) and then played the finals vs the meta (60 games per opponent).

Forge adoption rule: evidence: stages of 224 fresh paired games up to 896; adopt when the lower bound of the paired gain is above 1 point with at least 200 games, reject when the upper bound is below it, otherwise could not tell; a nominee with no gain after 2 stages (448 games) stops early as could not tell; α = 0.05 split over 4 looks (Bonferroni, z = 2.50). Each change it adopted, with its held-out paired gain, is in reports/forge/ and on the deck file's "// Forged" line; "could not tell" changes were not adopted.
