P7 check of the breeder's gate on the recorded bred decks: plan and analysis, written 2026-11-15 before any gate game was played.

What is tested
  The 16 decks bred in Seasons 1 and 2, as they are in the library today (their forged lists), each against the deck
  the gate would test it against today: its best parent by today's strength (the Hard power rating halfway to its
  lower bound), or for an explorer the middle meta deck by strength (YYB Rogue Gear).

  ("ratings now": the bred deck's power rating against the field of 47 decks minus its reference's, Hard, simulator
  version 9, 14 events; each rating's 90% range is about +/- 5 points. A different yardstick from the gate's, which is
  the paired difference against the meta decks.)

What is played
  Medium: five stages (1,400 paired games with 7 opponents, 1,200 with 6), no early stop, seed 7,000,000.
  Hard: two stages (560 or 480 paired games), no early stop, seed 7,100,000, for every pair (also the ones Medium
  shows worse, so the two bots can be compared).
  Simulator version 9. Nothing is recorded as an event, a rating, a ledger batch or an evidence row.

Questions, fixed in advance
  Q1  How many of the 16 would the gate keep out: by Medium, and by Hard among the rest.
  Q2  Does the gate agree with the ratings: sign agreement and rank correlation of the gate's difference with the
      rating difference; is every deck rated 10 or more points below its reference kept out; is any deck rated within
      5 points of its reference kept out.
  Q3  Do Medium and Hard agree for whole decks: sign agreement, the slope of the Hard difference on the Medium one,
      and any deck Medium shows worse whose Hard range lies entirely above zero.
  Q4  The per-game spread of the paired difference with each bot, against the 0.70 the tool assumes.
  Q5  The games the staged looks save against playing every stage.

Decisions, fixed in advance
  D1  If any deck is shown worse by Medium while its plain 95% range with Hard lies entirely above zero, or if Medium
      and Hard agree in sign for fewer than 12 of the 16, the default becomes Hard only (its cost stated); otherwise
      Medium first and Hard second stays.
  D2  If the median spread differs from 0.70 by more than 0.05 with either bot, the assumed spread becomes the measured
      median for that bot.
  D3  Whatever the numbers, the rule itself (bar at 0, enter unless shown worse, five looks and two looks) is not changed
      after seeing them. If it looks wrong, that is reported as a finding for the owner.
